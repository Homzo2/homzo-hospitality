Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a stay with HOMZO?
Booking is simple! Head over to the properties section on our homepage, select your preferred room, click 'Book Now', fill in your details, and confirm. Our team will reach out to verify and finalize.
What are the documents required for check-in?
You will need to present a government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar, Passport, or Voter ID). For student accommodations, college ID verification might be required. Foreign nationals must provide a valid passport and visa.
Can I cancel or modify my booking?
Yes, bookings can be cancelled or modified by contacting support. Free cancellation is generally available up to 24 hours prior to check-in, depending on individual property policies.
Is food included in the student/employee stays?
Many of our student stay properties offer optional daily meal plans. Check the amenities list under each specific property page to verify if food is included or available as an add-on.
