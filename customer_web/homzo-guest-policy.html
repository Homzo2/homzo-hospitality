General Booking Policy

Important

Certain destinations may have specific travel guidelines during particular periods of the year. All guests are required to comply with applicable local laws and regulations.

Policies are booking-specific and will be communicated to the guest either at the time of booking or upon check-in.

"Homzo" refers to Homzo Hospitality Pvt. Ltd., including its affiliates, employees, and officers. "Property" refers to the hotel or home where a valid booking has been made through Homzo.

🏨

Check-In Policy

  • The primary guest must be at least 18 years of age at the time of check-in.
  • Standard check-in time is 12:00 Noon. Guests may check in any time after that until the reservation is active.
  • A valid government-issued photo ID is mandatory for all guests above 18 years of age.
  • Accepted IDs: Aadhaar Card, Driving License, Voter ID, Passport. PAN card is not accepted.
  • Original ID must be presented — photocopies or digital copies may not be accepted.
⚠️ Check-in will be denied without a valid original government-issued photo ID.

If you face difficulty at check-in, Homzo will:

  1. Attempt to resolve the issue within the same property.
  2. Arrange alternate accommodation at another Homzo-listed property if available.
  3. Offer a full refund if no alternate accommodation can be arranged or accepted.
Homzo is not liable for compensation beyond the booking payment amount.

📅

Booking Extension

Extension of stay is subject to room availability at the current prevailing rate, which may differ from your original booking rate.

For long stays exceeding 7 nights, all outstanding payments must be settled on a weekly basis. Continued accommodation is conditional upon clearance of dues.

❌

Cancellation Policy

  • Bookings can be cancelled via the Homzo website or mobile app.
  • Applicable refunds will be processed within 7–14 working days.
  • Some properties do not accept bookings from unmarried couples or do not accept local ID proofs — this is disclosed prior to booking. Homzo will not be obligated to refund in such cases.
  • Properties reserve the right to deny check-in for invalid ID, unaccompanied minors, or suspicious activity. No refund obligation applies in such cases.
  • For corporate bookings, cancellation terms as per the signed contract will apply.
For booking-specific cancellation terms, refer to your booking confirmation voucher.
👨‍👩‍👧

Visitors & Child Policy

Visitors:

  • Day visitors are generally permitted in guest rooms.
  • Overnight visitors are not permitted.
  • All visitors must present valid government-issued photo ID at the front desk before accessing guest floors or rooms.

Children:

  • 1 child up to 5 years of age stays complimentary without an extra mattress.
  • Breakfast charges may apply for children.
🛏️

Triple Occupancy

Select properties allow triple occupancy with an extra mattress for the third guest at an additional charge. A full extra bed is generally not provided. Please confirm with the property before booking.

🐾

Pet Policy (Select Properties Only)

  • Only dogs and cats are permitted. Maximum 1 pet per booking.
  • Valid vaccination certificate required at check-in.
  • Pets must be on a leash in all common areas. Muzzles and poop bags must be carried at all times.
  • Pets are not allowed on beds, in the swimming pool, or in the restaurant.
  • Pet food is not available on-property.
  • Additional cleaning charges or penalties apply for any damage caused by pets.
  • Pets must not cause inconvenience to other guests.
⚖️

Code of Conduct

Homzo maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy for illegal activities across all properties. The property reserves the right to deny service or request eviction under the following circumstances:

  • Failure or refusal to pay for accommodation.
  • Disorderly behaviour disturbing the peace of other guests.
  • Damage, defacement, or threats to hotel property or fellow guests.
  • Any conduct deemed inappropriate by property management.
Guests may be held liable for any damage caused to property assets beyond normal wear and tear.

Smoking, Alcohol & Substances:

  • Smoking is prohibited in all Homzo Townhouse properties. Individual hotels may impose additional restrictions — check with the front desk.
  • Consumption of alcohol is prohibited in all public areas including lobbies, hallways, and parking areas.
  • Use or possession of substances listed under the NDPS Act, 1985 will result in immediate eviction and reporting to law enforcement.
🛡️

Safety & Security

  • Please follow all fire safety and emergency response procedures as directed by property staff.
  • Homzo and its partner properties are not liable for lost, misplaced, damaged, or stolen personal belongings or valuables.
  • An SOS emergency button is available within the Homzo app upon check-in for immediate assistance.
✅

Service On-Time Guarantee

Select Cities

At properties displaying the Service On-Time badge, Homzo guarantees resolution of any in-stay issue within 1 hour of reporting.

If unresolved, guests will be offered a room change within the same property or a transfer to an alternate Homzo property.

Refund amounts, if applicable, are determined based on the severity of the issue as per Homzo's internal guidelines.

Currently available in: Bengaluru, Kolkata, Delhi, Gurugram, Hyderabad, and Lucknow.
