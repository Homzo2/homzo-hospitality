Trust & Safety

Safe Stays. Trusted Experiences.

At Homzo, trust and safety are at the core of everything we do. We are committed to creating a secure, reliable, and comfortable environment for guests, property partners, and business associates across our hospitality network.

“Homzo is committed to building a trusted hospitality platform focused on safety, transparency, reliability, and customer satisfaction.”
🛡️

Guest Safety

To maintain a secure stay experience, Homzo encourages and implements:

  • Government ID Verification: Strict verification of valid government-issued photo identification during the check-in process.
  • Secure Bookings: End-to-end encrypted booking processes to safeguard user accounts and transactions.
  • Behavioral Standards: Clear guest guidelines that encourage safe, respectful, and neighbor-friendly behavior.
  • Emergency Support: Round-the-clock emergency support and rapid response coordinates for resolving critical situations.
Our goal is to ensure every guest feels safe and comfortable throughout their stay.
🏢

Property Verification

Homzo works only with property partners who comply with essential hospitality and operational standards, including:

  • Hygiene & Cleanliness: Mandatory regular sanitization and immaculate maintenance of rooms, beds, and restrooms.
  • Proper Housekeeping: Professional upkeep, functional appliances, and structured housekeeping processes.
  • Transparent Listings: Absolute accuracy in room details, amenities checklist, and pricing sheets without hidden fees.
  • Service Standards: Guest-friendly support protocols and warm, welcoming service from on-ground staff.
⚠️ Properties that fail to maintain quality and safety standards may face immediate suspension or permanent removal from the platform.
🔒

Privacy & Data Security

We value customer privacy and take extensive technical and administrative measures to protect all user data from unauthorized access or disclosure.


Customer data is utilized strictly for:

  • Seamless booking management and invoicing.
  • Providing dedicated, helpful customer support.
  • Safety verification, fraud prevention, and profile audit processes.
  • Constant optimization and enhancements of our platform services.
🛡️ Homzo does not sell, rent, or trade personal customer information to unauthorized third parties.
🚫

Responsible Platform Usage

To maintain a trusted hospitality ecosystem, the following activities are strictly prohibited:

  • ❌ Fraudulent Bookings: Use of stolen financial instruments or identity spoofing to secure reservations.
  • ❌ Illegal Activities: Any law-breaking activities or substance abuse at partner properties.
  • ❌ Platform Misuse: Attempting to bypass security infrastructure or scrape listings information.
  • ❌ Hostile Behavior: Harassment, violence, or unsafe conduct towards property staff, fellow guests, or neighbors.
⚠️ Violation of these standards may result in immediate account suspension, booking cancellation, forfeiture of payments, or reporting to law enforcement authorities.
🤝

Partner Responsibility

Homzo believes in building professional, transparent, and ethical partnerships. Our property partners are core pillars of this trusted platform and are expected to:


  • Maintain and enforce maximum guest safety standards on-property.
  • Provide perfectly accurate, up-to-date property specifications and photos.
  • Deliver highly respectful, empathetic, and professional customer service.
  • Strictly adhere to local municipal laws, building rules, and hospitality guidelines.
