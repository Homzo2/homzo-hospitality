Executive Leadership

Rishabh Kumar Modanwal

Founder & CEO

Originally from Fatehpur, Uttar Pradesh, Rishabh Kumar Modanwal is the Founder and CEO of Homzo. His entrepreneurial journey represents a story of ground-level experience, operational adaptability, and passion for excellence.

"Our goal at Homzo is not just to rent spaces, but to build a reliable, world-class hospitality platform where trust, safety, and luxury comfort are accessible to everyone."

Rishabh began his professional career in 2021 within the real estate administration sector, developing deep knowledge in property operations and client services. Backed by local expertise, he embarked on his first entrepreneurial venture, starting R.K Security Services, where he gained valuable experience in team leadership and risk management.

To further refine his operational expertise, he joined Qs Metrology in Gurugram as a Store Assistant (March 2024 – September 2025), optimizing inventories and store management protocols. Continuing his career trajectory, he served as a Health Manager at Hindustan Wellness (October 2025 – December 2026), managing healthcare client profiles and supervising administrative logistics.

Leveraging these diverse operational and management insights, he launched Homzo to transform the Indian hospitality and rental booking landscape, aiming to build a scalable and premium brand that puts customer care first.

This Policy applies to all users of our Platform, including guests, registered members, and visitors. By accessing or using our Platform, you consent to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this Policy, please discontinue use of our Platform. 2. Data Storage & Security Your data is stored on secure servers located within India, in compliance with the Information Technology Act, 2000, and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 ("SPDI Rules"). We implement industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These include: Secure Socket Layer (SSL) / Transport Layer Security (TLS) encryption for data in transit AES-256 encryption for sensitive data at rest Role-based access controls and multi-factor authentication for internal systems Regular security audits, vulnerability assessments, and penetration testing Employee training on data privacy and security protocols However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security. 6.
