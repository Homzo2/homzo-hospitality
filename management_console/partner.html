Frequently Asked Questions

When and how are platform payouts processed?

Payouts are processed bi-weekly (on the 10th and 25th of every month) for completed bookings. Earnings are transferred via direct NEFT/IMPS to your verified bank account once KYC verification is completed.

What compliance documents are required for KYC verification?

Partners must provide Aadhaar Card (front and back), PAN Card, and Cancelled Cheque/Passbook showing the bank account holder name and IFSC. For registered firms, GSTIN certificate is also verified.

How can I request cancellation for a booking or report guest damage?

Select the Booking from the reservations list to manage cancellations, or submit an urgent case under "Guest Dispute & Damage" within 24 hours with incident details. You can also message our WhatsApp concierge directly.
