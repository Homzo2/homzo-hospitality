Frequently Asked Questions

When do payouts get processed?

Payouts are processed bi-weekly (on the 10th and 25th of every month) for bookings completed. Earnings are transferred directly to your bank account once verification is completed.

How can I request cancellation for a booking?

Select the Booking from the reservations list, click the Action button, and click Cancel. Homzo cancellation policies apply depending on guest type and check-in timeline.
