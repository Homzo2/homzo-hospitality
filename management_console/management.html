Internal FAQs & Policies
What is the policy for cancellation?

Full refund before 48 hours for general stays. Stricter rules apply for month-long student and employee tenancies.

How are partner commissions computed?

The base commission is 15% of the room subtotal, unless a specific city-wide or partner override is defined.
